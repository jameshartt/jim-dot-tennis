# Captain guide

This guide follows a captain's week. A captain can see and work with every team in the club, not just their own. The club works as one squad.

Related guides: the [administrator guide](guide-admin.md) for accounts and seasons, and the [player sheet](guide-player.md) to send to your players.

## Sign in and find your teams

1. Open the web address of the app and sign in with your passkey, or with your username and password.
2. You land on the **Captain Dashboard**. It lists what you can do, grouped under League Management and Results and Standings.
3. Choose **Captain Planning**.
4. At the top right, press **Link to a player** and choose yourself. The button then says "You are:" with your name. Your own teams are now marked with a star, and a **My Teams** button appears.

The **My Teams** button is a shortcut so you see only your own teams. It never hides anything from you. Press **All Teams** to see everything again.

## See who is available

Players set their own availability from their personal links. You see the result on the planning page.

1. Choose **Captain Planning**.
2. Pick the **Week** with **Prev** and **Next** or the week list.
3. Open "Availability for this week". Each team shows how many men and women are available out of how many it needs, and how many players have not replied. The label says **Enough players** or **Short on players**.
4. Read the grid below it. Each row is a player and each column is a fixture. The marks mean:
   - A green dot: available.
   - An amber half dot: if needed. The player can make it work if selection is tight.
   - A red dot: unavailable.
   - A grey circle: no reply yet.

On a phone the grid is narrow. The page suggests rotating your phone or using a tablet or computer. Press **View anyway** to keep going.

## Ask players for their availability

If a team is short, press the **Remind** button beside it in "Availability for this week". It sends a reminder to the players who have not replied and have turned on notifications.

Two other places do the same job:

- **Remind All** on the players page and the fixtures page reminds every active player who has not set next week.
- **Remind Availability** on a fixture's page reminds the players who have not updated for that match.

Players who have not turned on notifications will not receive a reminder. The players page shows who has them on. For those players, a message in the team chat works well.

## Pick the team

There are two ways. Use whichever suits you.

**From the planning grid.** Click a player's cell under a fixture to add them to that team's selection. Click again to take them out. A tick shows who is in.

**From the fixture.** Open the fixture and press **Select Team Players**. You can also reach it from **Selection Overview** on the dashboard, with **Select Team**, **Select Home Team** or **Select Away Team**.

1. Under "Available Players", press a player's name to add them. They appear under "Selected Players". "Players Selected" counts up to 8.
2. Drag players, or tap them, into the matchups: **1st Mixed**, **2nd Mixed**, **Mens** and **Womens**. Each takes two players.
3. Press **Select Day Captain** to mark who captains on the day.
4. Tap a player in a matchup to take them out.

Names are coloured by availability: available, if needed, unavailable and unknown.

## How the league rules show on screen

The selection page checks the two main league rules for you. Each player's button has a symbol and a tooltip that explains it.

**One team a week.** A player should play for one team in a Monday to Friday week. If someone is already picked for another team that week, their button shows a warning triangle and says "Already played this week", with the team name. It is a prompt, so you can still choose them if you need to. The fixture page also warns when a player is in two matchups of the same fixture.

**Playing down.** In the second half of the season (from week 10 of 18, and for fixtures moved into the second half) a player who has played five or more matches for a higher team cannot play for a lower one. The page counts only matches that have been completed. Teams are ranked by name: the club name first, then A, B and so on. You see:

- A tick: eligible. The button says how many matches are left before the player is locked to this team, for example "2 before locked", or "locked after this".
- A padlock: eligible here, but locked to this team. They cannot drop to a lower team.
- A forbidden sign: not eligible. They are locked to a higher team, with its name. The button is greyed out.

Before the second half, none of this applies.

## Tell the players

Once the team is picked, open the fixture.

1. Press **Notify Selected Players**. Confirm. Each selected player who has notifications on gets a message.
2. For everyone else, use the fixture summary. Click it to copy the details, then paste it into your team chat. The **Share** button on the fixture page does the same job.
3. **Download Calendar** gives a calendar file for the match.

## Enter the result

After the match, you have two options.

**Enter it yourself.**

1. Open the fixture and press **Enter Results**.
2. For each matchup, type the games for set 1 and set 2. Tick "3rd set played" to add a third set.
3. If a matchup was not played, tick "Matchup conceded (no play)" and say who conceded. If a match started and then stopped, tick "Match retired (play started, then stopped)" and say who retired.
4. Press **Save Results & Complete Fixture**.

To correct a result later, open the fixture and press **Edit Results**.

**Import from BHPLTA.** Choose **Match Card Import** on the dashboard, choose the week, tick **Dry Run Mode** to check first, then press **Start Import**. The administrator guide explains the options.

## Move a fixture

Matches get rained off. Here is how to record a new date.

1. Open the fixture and press **Edit Schedule**. (From the dashboard banner or the fixtures page, press **Reschedule**.)
2. Choose the **New Scheduled Date**. It must be inside the season.
3. Pick a **Reason for Change**: Weather, Court Availability or Other. Add notes if you like.
4. Press **Save Changes**.

The fixture now shows the status **Rescheduled**. The selected team is cleared, so pick it again nearer the new date. The old dates are kept in the schedule history.

A fixture that passes its date with no result shows **Awaiting Reschedule**. It appears in an orange list on the fixtures page and as a banner on the dashboard. Set a new date so players show as available again.

## Keep notes and names tidy

- **Captain notes.** Press the note icon next to a player on the planning grid to write a private note. Players cannot see it.
- **Preferred Name Approvals.** When a player asks to be called something else on team sheets, approve or reject it here.
- **Points Table** shows how the league stands.

## If something looks wrong

Pages that only administrators can use show a short message with a link back to the dashboard. Ask an administrator, or see the [administrator guide](guide-admin.md).

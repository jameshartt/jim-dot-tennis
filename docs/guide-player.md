# Your link

Welcome to the team! Your captain has sent you a personal link. It is all you need. There is no account to make and no password to remember.

## Open your page

Tap your link. You will see your upcoming matches and a calendar. It works in any browser on any phone or computer.

Your link is yours alone. Save it somewhere handy, such as in a message to yourself, or use "Bookmark this page" on your page.

## Say when you can play

Your captain picks the team from who is free, so every reply helps.

1. Tap a date on the calendar to change it. Each date can be Available, If Needed or Unavailable. Dates you have not touched show as Not Set.
2. "If Needed" means you can make it work if the team is short, but would rather not.
3. Looking ahead is easy. You can set up to four weeks. Checking in once a week is perfect.

Handy buttons:

- **Set Next Week Available** or **Set Next Week Unavailable** answers for the whole week in one tap.
- **Mark Time Away** covers a holiday or a busy spell in one go. Choose the dates and the status, and add a note if you like.

## Get a nudge when you are picked

Turn on notifications and your phone tells you when you are selected, and when your captain would love to hear your availability.

1. At the top of your page, tap **Enable Notifications**.
2. Allow notifications when your phone asks.
3. Tap **Test** to see one arrive.

On an iPhone, first add the page to your home screen (see below), then open it from there and tap **Enable Notifications**.

## Put it on your phone

Having your page one tap away makes it easy to keep up to date.

**iPhone (Safari):** tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

**Android (Chrome):** tap the menu with the three dots, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

Your page opens straight from the icon, as your own page.

## A few extras

- **Request Preferred Name** lets you choose how your name appears on team sheets. Your captain or administrator approves it.
- If your club has switched it on, **Tell us about your tennis** helps captains build pairings that bring out your best.
- If your club shares an end-of-season review, you will find a way to open it on your page.

Thank you for being part of the team. See you on court!

You do not choose a password. Creating the user gives you an invite link to pass on; the person who opens it sets up their own passkey or password.

A captain runs every team: planning, availability, selection, fixtures and rescheduling, results, players and standings. An admin can do all of that and also manage users, sessions, club settings, seasons, imports and backups. Players do not need an account: they use their own availability link. Link a captain to their player record so their own teams are marked on the planning dashboard.

Tournaments only is for people who run the club's tournaments and nothing else: they sign in like everyone else and go straight to tournament management, with no league pages. Choose own tournaments (the ones they run or are given) or all tournaments. A captain has no tournament access unless you give it here. Administrators always have every tournament.{{if .TournamentSets}} The club runs more than one set of tournaments: tick the ones each person can manage. The ticks only matter for someone whose Tournaments is not "No tournaments".{{end}}

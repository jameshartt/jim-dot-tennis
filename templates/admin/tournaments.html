Sets of tournaments

A club can keep separate sets of tournaments, for example one for the club's own tournaments and one for a league cup. Each has its own tournaments and its own list of people who can manage it. Most clubs need only one.

Add another set of tournaments

This is what people see when they choose which tournaments to manage, for example "Parks Cup". 2 to 12 letters or digits. It cannot be changed later. If the tournament server already has tournaments under this code, they are taken on as they are, with their tournaments.

Adding one gives it to nobody except administrators. Choose who can manage it on the users page.

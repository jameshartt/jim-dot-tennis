Use the fixtures on the BHPLTA website

This reads the Parks League fixtures page and the club pages on the BHPLTA website and creates, in one go:

  • the season the fixtures belong to, with its weeks,
  • its divisions and every team in them,
  • all the fixtures,
  • any club this site does not know yet, with its venue details. Clubs already here are reused, never duplicated.

Use it when there is no previous season here to copy. If a previous season exists, copying it from the season's setup page also carries players and captains over.

BHPLTA only lists upcoming fixtures: run this before the season starts to get every week. If anything fails, nothing is saved and you can try again.
